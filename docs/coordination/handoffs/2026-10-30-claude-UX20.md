# UX20: kitchen screen light mode

## What changed
- The kitchen no longer forces the `dark` class. It follows the theme chosen in Screen tools, like every other screen.
- `kitchen.css`: every colour with a meaning is now a `--k-*` token. Dark values sit on `.kitchen.kitchen` and are unchanged; light values sit on `:root[data-theme=light] .kitchen.kitchen`. The chosen colour in light is the app's teal, as in Admin.
- The Sunlight switch now also strengthens the kitchen. A locked kitchen gets a matching page background (`shell.css`).
- The Undo button keeps its own amber and red in both themes.

## How to test
Screen tools → Appearance → Light, then open Kitchen in Three steps and One tap, with a burger with extras and leave-offs, an order note, and a ready order.

## Checks
`npm run check` and `npm run e2e`. Screenshots at 1180x820 and 390x844 in both themes and both flows, plus locked: no overflow, no small targets. The warm and late bands were forced in the screenshot scenario because a fresh order has no age. Undo was not photographed in light.

## Gotchas
- A device on Appearance: System with Windows or the tablet set to light now gets the light kitchen, where it was always dark before.
- The Sunlight colours were not photographed.

## Follow-ups
None. Not in rc.4; it needs a new build. No new dependency, no contract change.
