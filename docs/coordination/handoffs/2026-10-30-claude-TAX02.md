# Handoff: TAX02 — PyroBookKeeper removed; "Charge ABB" switch
- Agent: claude   Date: 2026-10-30   Branch: agent/claude/TAX01 (continued)

Two owner requests on 2026-10-30, after TAX01: "remove pyro bookkeeping entirely" (confirmed: only the
PyroBookKeeper link, keep the CSV exports), and a switch in the Tax tab to turn ABB off, because Allan's dad says
food trucks ("trukipan") are exempt under a special rule.

## What changed
- **PyroBookKeeper removed** (ADR-0003): `books-bridge/src/pbk.ts`, `POST /api/books/pbk/post`, the `pbkFolder`
  setting, the PyroBookKeeper section of Admin > Bookkeeping and its tests. `dayBooking`, `days.csv` and
  `journal.csv` stay. Comments, contracts, PLAN, AGENTS lane and BOARD T34 updated.
- **Charge ABB switch**: `TaxExemption` and `effectiveTaxCategories` in `shared/tax.ts`; `PosService.taxSetup()`
  and `taxCategories()` (now the effective view) in `service.ts`; `AdminService.setTaxExemption`;
  `GET /api/admin/tax`, `PUT /api/admin/tax-exemption`. Tax tab: switch at the top; switching off asks for the reason.
- Receipt (`receipt/src/build.ts`): "Prices include ABB" only when the sale has a taxed row. Menu board: footer says
  "Prices in USD" without "include ABB" when no category is taxed.
- Tests: `shared/tax.test.ts` (exemption), `server/taxExemption.test.ts` (new sales exempt, earlier sales keep ABB,
  reason required, owner only), `e2e/tax-admin.mjs` (switch in the browser).

## Contracts touched
- New routes and `TaxExemption` (http.md, money-and-tax.md). `GET /api/books` no longer returns `pbk`;
  `BooksSettings` no longer has `pbkFolder` (books.md). `MenuResponse` shape unchanged.

## How to verify
- `npm run check`; `npm run build && node e2e/run.mjs tax-admin` (screenshots `tax-abb-off*.png`).

## Gotchas / decisions
- The switch encodes no tax rule. Whether a food truck qualifies, and what "as long as they pay cash" means, is
  QUESTIONS Q1b (reopened). Default stays ABB on.
- While ABB is off, `/api/menu` shows every category as exempt (named "ABB 6% (exempt)" etc.). Anything that edits
  categories must read `GET /api/admin/tax`, never write back what `/api/menu` returned.
- Old databases may still hold `pbkFolder` and `books.pbkDeviceId`; they are ignored, not deleted.
- The POS cart still shows "incl. ABB $0.00" while ABB is off: `Pos.tsx` is being changed by Codex (REL08), so I
  left it alone. Readiness (`readiness.ts`, on Codex's branch) still counts unverified categories while ABB is off.
