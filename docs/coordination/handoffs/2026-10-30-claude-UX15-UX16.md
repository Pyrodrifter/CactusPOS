# Handoff: UX15 + UX16 — admin clean-up, kitchen for greasy fingers
- Agent: claude   Date: 2026-10-30   Branch: claude/amazing-ramanujan-kne4ff

Owner request: work on UI/UX; clean up the admin screen for readability and layout, and make the kitchen screen
easier for a cook with greasy fingers.

## What changed
**Kitchen (UX16, `apps/kitchen/`)**
- **Wipe screen** button on the kitchen header. It opens the existing 20-second cleaning pause from Screen tools
  (which was two taps deep). The shell listens for `CLEAN_SCREEN_EVENT` (`lib/display.ts`); this is the only
  web-core change, a few lines in `ui/AppShell.tsx`.
- **Only a deliberate tap moves a ticket on.** A press held longer than 0.7 s (a thumb steadying the tablet) or one
  that slides more than 16 px (a smear) is ignored, on the whole ticket and on its buttons. Keyboard presses
  (`detail === 0`) always count, so `element.click()` in the e2e tools still works.
- Options (Extra cheese, Hot) and line notes are larger and nearly as bright as the item; bigger ticket number and
  quantity badge. Recall is outlined and 20 px from Collected.
- On phones the "Kitchen" heading is hidden (the highlighted Kitchen button sits just above it) and the connection
  pill is reduced to its green Wi-Fi sign, so Wipe fits. Reconnecting keeps its word.

**Admin (UX15, `apps/admin/`)**
- The nine sections are grouped: **Every day** (Today, Menu), **Set up** (Receipt design, Business, Staff, Tax,
  Printer), **Records** (Bookkeeping, Backups). At 1100 px and wider the same `<nav>` becomes a sticky side menu
  (CSS only, one DOM); narrower it stays the sideways row, with thin dividers between groups.
- Every page opens with its name as a heading plus the one-line description. Hidden visually on phones (the chosen
  tab sits right above) and on Receipt design (the editor has its own header); always there for screen readers.
- `notice info`: a calm grey-blue style for notes that only explain (Tax intro, Printer intro). Amber is kept for
  something to act on (unpaid orders, example PINs).
- Tighter notices on phones; tax rows let their buttons wrap under the text instead of squeezing it; beside the side
  menu the category Delete keeps only its red bin (aria-label unchanged), so the Dutch menu tools stay on one row.

**Elsewhere**
- `e2e/kitchen-taps.mjs` (new, added to `e2e/run.mjs`): short tap moves on, resting thumb and smear do not, Wipe pauses touch.
- `docs/manual/shots.mjs` selects admin tabs by `data-tab` instead of `:nth-child` (the group labels are siblings now).
- Manual EN + NL: one paragraph in the Kitchen section about short taps and **Wipe screen** / **Scherm afnemen**.
  Retook the pictures of the screens that changed (kitchen, admin pages, close-day); the rest are untouched.

## Contracts touched
None. No routes, events or schemas changed. New DOM hooks: `.admin-tabs button[data-tab]`, `.admin-tabs-group`,
`.admin-page-head`, `.kitchen-wipe`.

## How to verify
```bash
npm run check
npm run e2e
```
All 7 browser checks pass, including `kitchen-taps.mjs`. In a browser: Kitchen, tap **Wipe screen** (touch pauses
20 s); press and hold a New ticket for a second, nothing happens; a short tap starts it. Admin at 1180x820 shows the
side menu, at 1024x700 the grouped row. `page.measure()` is clean on every admin page and the kitchen at the four
check sizes, light and dark.

## Gotchas / decisions
- **Side menu vs. owner's UX08 preference.** The owner asked for top navigation back in UX08; that was about the
  app-wide navigation. Admin's own sections becoming a side menu on a wide screen is a different thing, but if the
  owner dislikes it, removing the `@media (min-width: 1100px)` block in `admin.css` restores the row exactly.
- The tap thresholds (700 ms, 16 px) are a judgement call; they live at the top of `Kitchen.tsx`. Start/Ready are
  still not double-confirmed: Undo covers Ready and Collected as before.
- The manual pictures here were taken with Chromium on Linux, so their font differs slightly from the Windows Edge
  pictures beside them. Re-run `node scripts/make-manual.mjs --shots` on the Windows laptop before the next release
  to make them match. (`make-manual` printing the PDF failed in this Linux container; the pictures were written.)
- Running the screenshot tools as root on Linux needs Chromium with `--no-sandbox` (a two-line wrapper in
  `SHOT_BROWSER`); nothing in the repo was changed for that.
