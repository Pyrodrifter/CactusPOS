## What changed
- Production builds include a standalone manifest, 192/512px normal/maskable icons, an Apple icon and a service worker with a content-versioned inventory of every generated app asset.
- Added browser registration and install/update controls with English and Dutch text. An update waits for staff to choose and confirm reload; other open screens keep running.

## Gotchas / decisions
- Offline shell availability does not allow sales while the laptop is unreachable. Unsent carts remain in an open screen only; a reload clears them and the update confirmation explicitly explains that.
- Safe write replay is deferred to shared/server coordination request R6 because a missing response may follow an already committed order/payment. T05 itself remains in review.
- Existing app caches are retained while windows remain open to support older running bundles after another tab approves an update. Cleanup touches only app-owned caches when a later activation has no windows.
- T07 still owns catalog consolidation; the new bilingual PWA messages are scoped beside the shell.
- Desktop browser validation cannot establish physical phone certificate trust, WiFi or home-screen installation; T06's phone drill remains pending.
