# Handoff: UX14 — correct a paid order in one step
- Agent: claude   Date: 2026-10-10   Branch: agent/claude/correct-paid

Owner request: "a way to edit orders after they have been paid instead of voiding it if we made a mistake".

## What changed
- **Till:** every paid order in Orders has a **Correct** button. One sheet: change quantities, take items off (down
  to 0), add items from the menu, switch cash and card, say what went wrong. It shows what the order was, what it
  becomes, and what to do with the money ("Give $3.00 back from the drawer", "Charge $1.50 more on the card machine").
- **Server:** `POST /api/orders/:id/correct` (`PosService.correctOrder`). A paid order is never rewritten:
  - the original is voided with the reason and gets `correctedBy`;
  - a replacement sale is created and paid in the same transaction, on the **next receipt number**, with
    `corrects` pointing back; it keeps the ticket number and the kitchen state;
  - lines that stay keep the name, price and tax they were sold at; added lines are priced from the menu now.
- **Day report:** a correction is not a refund. The original counts like a refunded sale and the replacement like a
  sale, so sales, ABB, payments and the drawer move by the difference only. Corrections are listed on their own
  (`DayReport.corrections`), on the printed report and under Admin → Today.
- **Receipts:** the new one says "Replaces receipt #N"; a reprint of the old one says VOID and "Corrected: see receipt #M".
- Files: `shared/src/{domain,reports}.ts`, `server/src/{service,app,reports}.ts`, `receipt/src/{build,dayReport}.ts`,
  `web/src/apps/pos/{Pos.tsx,pos.css}`, `web/src/apps/admin/ReportsAdmin.tsx`, tests, `e2e/correct-order.mjs`.

No new dependency. No database migration (the links live in the order JSON and its events).

## Contracts touched
`contracts/http.md`: the new route, `CorrectOrderBody`, `CorrectOrderResponse`, the `Idempotency-Key` section and the
day-report paragraph. `Order` gained optional `corrects` and `correctedBy`; `DayReport` gained optional
`corrections { count, removedCents, addedCents }`; `orders.refunded` and `refunds` no longer include corrected orders.

## Gotchas / decisions
- **One sale can use two receipt numbers** (the first void, the second the corrected sale). That is deliberate: a
  receipt that was handed out cannot change afterwards. Whether the accountant wants it this way is Q30.
- Who may correct: the owner, or staff with the manager PIN, the same rule as voiding a paid order.
- The till does not move money. For card the cashier refunds or charges the difference on the card machine; the
  sheet says how much. With a drawer and a receipt printer set up, saving a cash correction opens the drawer and
  prints the new receipt, as a normal payment does.
- A correction made on a later day lands on that day (rule from Q11): the closed day is not reopened.
- Everything left on a corrected order has to be at least one item. Taking everything off is a void, and the sheet says so.
- The kitchen sees the corrected items when the order is still on its screen (the replacement is a new order to the
  screen, so it beeps). Nothing is sent to the kitchen for an order that was already collected.
- Unpaid orders are not corrected this way: add a round, or void.
