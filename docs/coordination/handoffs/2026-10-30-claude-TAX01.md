## Gotchas / decisions
- **There is deliberately no "add ABB at checkout" mode.** Bonaire does not allow offering taxed goods and services
  to consumers exclusive of ABB. The switch only changes how the owner types prices: what is stored, shown and
  charged is always the inclusive price, so the order, receipt, reports and books paths are untouched.
- An untouched price field keeps its stored cents in "without ABB" mode. Not every inclusive price can be reached
  from a whole-cent net (e.g. some prices would move a cent on the way back), so the editor never re-derives a price
  the owner did not change.
- The kind of a saved category (taxed / exempt / outside scope) cannot be changed, for the same reason rates are
  dated rows: earlier sales must keep adding up. Make a new category instead.
- Categories cannot be deleted. Unused ones show "No menu items".
- Sanity check of the sample Cactus Blue menu at 6% included: e.g. Cactus Double $19.75 = $18.63 + $1.12 ABB; a cart
  of Cactus Double, 2 soft drinks and chips = $27.25 with $1.54 ABB (per-line rounding). Whether the resold chips
  and bottled drinks are 6% or outside scope is still Q1b for her accountant.
- PyroBookKeeper integration was left out on the owner's request.
