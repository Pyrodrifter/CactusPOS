## Gotchas / decisions

- Root certificate sharing/trust is manual. Regenerate leaf whenever IP/hostname changes.
- Documentation consulted: https://github.com/FiloSottile/mkcert (custom cert/key paths, no -install) and https://caddyserver.com/docs/caddyfile/directives/reverse_proxy (WebSocket forwarding).
