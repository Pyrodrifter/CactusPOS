### Definitions (the books bridge T34 should rely on these)
- **Sales** = paid orders that are not void, by `business_date` (the day the order was created).
- **Refunded** = orders voided after payment. Not in sales, tenders or ABB; shown separately. They keep their receipt number, so the receipt range includes them.
- **Tenders** = money for sales that stand, per method, tips separate. `sum(tenders) = sales gross`.
- **Cash expected** = cash tender total. A cash sale that was refunded nets to nothing. Excludes any float.
- **ABB** = sum of each receipt's stored per-line ABB, grouped by category and rate. Same rounding as the receipts.
- A day can be closed more than once; each close has a new number and the latest per date is authoritative.
