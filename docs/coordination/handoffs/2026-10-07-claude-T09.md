## What changed
- `seed/cactus-blue.json`: the 2025-2026 menu board from https://www.cactusblue.us/our-menu/ (15 items, 5 categories, shared option groups), business address and brand colour. Business-specific data lives here, not in code.
- server `seedProfile.ts`: `applyProfile(db, name)` replaces menu + business details from `seed/<name>.json`; staff, tax setup and orders are untouched. `npm run seed -- cactus-blue`, or `CACTUS_SEED=cactus-blue` on first start.
- Market-price items: the cashier types the price per sale (Lion Fish Burger). Server rejects the order without one.
- Optional item description (shown in the option sheet) and optional business brand colour (top bar).

## Contracts touched (additive)
- `MenuItem.openPrice` (default false), `MenuItem.description?`
- `OrderLineInput.priceCents?` — required for, and only honoured on, `openPrice` items
- `BusinessSchema.brandColor?` (`#rrggbb`)

## How to verify
`npm run check` (22 tests). `CACTUS_SEED=cactus-blue` on an empty DB, sign in, open Lion Fish, tap the burger: Add stays disabled until a price is typed.
Verified in a browser: real menu renders, market price 22.50 gives a $22.50 line with $1.27 ABB.

## Gotchas / decisions
- All items default to tax category `service_6` (unverified). Chips and bottled drinks are resold imported goods and may be outside ABB scope: see QUESTIONS Q1b. Do not change without the accountant.
- "All items come with spinach & tomato" is modelled as a "Without" option group; the $1 extras, +$5 patty and +$3 salad as an "Extras" group on every burger and wrap.
- Sodas, water and chips are one item each with a required "Which one" choice, so the kitchen ticket says which.
- Not on the menu board, so not seeded: Lion Fish wrap and the "Flamingo Droppings" dessert mentioned on the home page (QUESTIONS Q5b).
- `applyProfile` deletes and reloads the menu tables; it is an explicit command, never automatic on a non-empty DB.
