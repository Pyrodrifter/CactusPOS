## Gotchas
- **PyroBookKeeper's ABB return ignores these postings** (it is built from invoices). Stated in the UI, the contract and the ADR.
- The PyroBookKeeper folder must be on the till computer (or a synced folder visible to it).
- Nothing posts automatically; the owner presses the button.
- Account defaults are PyroBookKeeper's chart. Card takings default to the bank account 1100; a bookkeeper may prefer a clearing account.
- Not tried against the owner's live PyroBookKeeper data folder, only a throwaway one.
