**Release candidate.** For trying out on the truck before 0.3.0. Keep 0.2.0 at hand if something is wrong; your
sales, menu and settings stay where they are either way.

## Install or update (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

Same as 0.2.0: download **`CactusPOS-Setup-0.3.0-rc.1.exe`**, double-click, **Next**, **Next**, **Finish**. Sales,
menu, PINs and settings are kept. Windows warns about the unsigned file: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Kitchen

- **One tap per order.** Everything still to cook is on one board, oldest first, with no empty columns. Tap a ticket
  when the food is done: it goes straight to **Ready**, also on the pickup board. Ready orders shrink to their number
  in a green strip at the top; tap the number when the guest has it. **Undo** takes back either tap.
- **Three steps** (New, Preparing, Ready) is still there for whoever prefers it: Screen tools → Kitchen screen.
- **Lock this screen to the kitchen** (Screen tools → Kitchen screen): the tablet always opens on the kitchen and
  Order, Admin and the bar at the top are gone, so nobody switches away by accident. Switch it off in the same place.
- **Made for greasy fingers**: only a short tap counts, so a thumb resting on the screen or a smear does not move a
  ticket; **Wipe screen** at the top pauses touch for 20 seconds; options and notes larger and brighter.

## Admin

- Sections grouped as **Every day**, **Set up** and **Records**; on a landscape tablet they are a menu down the left.
- Every page opens with its name and what it is for; notes that only explain are calm, amber is kept for what needs
  doing.
- **Tax (ABB)** works for any Bonaire business: add categories from presets (6%, 8%, 7%, 0%, exempt), rename them,
  and a **Tax check** that flags a missing or unverified rate. Menu prices can be typed **without ABB**; the till
  adds it and shows what the customer pays.
- **Charge ABB** switch for a business that does not charge ABB. Switching off asks for a reason; earlier sales keep
  their ABB. Whether a food truck qualifies is still a question for the accountant, so it stays on by default.
- The PyroBookKeeper link is removed. The bookkeeping CSV exports are unchanged.

## Checked, and not checked

Checked in this build's code: 254 unit tests and 8 browser checks pass (including the one-tap kitchen, the lock and
the greasy-finger taps); every Admin page and the kitchen fit 1180×820, 1024×700, 820×1180 and 390×844 in light and
dark.

Not yet checked: the one-tap kitchen and the lock on the real kitchen tablet during service; the setup of this
version on Windows beyond the build computer. The manual's new kitchen and Admin pictures were taken on Linux and
look slightly different in font from the others.
