**CactusPOS 0.3.0**: everything from the tryout on the truck, made ready for every day. Coming from 0.2.0 or a
release candidate, your sales, menu, PINs and settings are kept.

## Install or update (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

Download **`CactusPOS-Setup-0.3.0.exe`**, double-click, **Next**, **Next**, **Finish**. Sales,
menu, PINs and settings are kept. Windows warns about the unsigned file: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New since the tryout (since rc.5)

- **Ready orders on the Order screen.** Orders the kitchen has finished show above the cart, with the name or number
  and what is in them. Tap **Picked up** when the guest has it (**Undo** for a few seconds), or **Pay** if it was not
  paid yet.
- **A name instead of a number.** Type the guest's name in **Name for pickup** in the cart; the pickup board shows that
  name. Without a name it shows the number as before.
- **Stock.** The **Stock** button on the Order screen: count what you prepared for today and see how much is sold and
  left, and keep a count of ingredients by hand. Whole numbers; a Cactus Double counts as one burger.
- **No ABB to pay.** With Charge ABB switched off, Admin → Today says so, with the reason, and the cart no longer
  shows "incl. ABB $0.00".
- **Picked up by hand by default.** Ready orders no longer disappear by themselves after a minute, so they wait on the
  Order screen until someone taps them. A till where a time was chosen keeps it (Screen tools → Kitchen screen).
- **Tidier Order screen**: service switch beside the search, compact cart lines, lower menu tiles.
- **Fixed**: item descriptions on the menu tiles were cut through the middle of their second line.
- **Fixed**: three known (moderate) security problems in a part the till uses to serve its pages.
- The manual's pictures show the screens of this version.

## Also new since 0.2.0

- **Kitchen in light mode.** The kitchen follows the Light or Dark choice in Screen tools. A device on System follows
  its own setting, so set a kitchen tablet to Dark if it should stay dark.
- **A simpler menu creator.** One price, the one the customer pays, and no ABB fields. ABB is worked out in the
  background and set once under Admin → Tax. Choosing where an item is prepared is gone too.
- **Allergies and diet.** Tap Gluten, Milk, Egg, Nuts, Peanuts, Soy, Fish, Shellfish, Sesame, Vegetarian, Vegan or Spicy
  on an item. They show on the till and the menu board. A guest's own allergy still goes in the order note.
- **Your own category colours.** Admin → Menu: tap one of 8 colours for a category and the till follows.
- **Kitchen tickets are easier to read.** A band on top shows the number, the name and the wait (amber, then red).
  A choice sits next to the item (*Dutchie Burger · Curry sauce*), extras are green **+** tags and leave-offs red
  **NO** tags, side by side instead of one line each. Paid / unpaid is no longer shown in the kitchen.
- **Picked up by itself, if you want.** Under Screen tools → Kitchen screen a ready order can leave the kitchen and
  the pickup board after 1, 2 or 5 minutes. Off by default.
- **Tax is set once for the whole menu**, under Admin → Tax (**Use for the whole menu**). The 8% ABB category is gone; a till that never sold under it loses it by itself.
- **Leave the ABB lines off the receipt**: Receipt design → Payment details → **Show ABB lines**. The total stays;
  reports keep the ABB. Ask the accountant before switching it off.
- **Tidier**: a clearer Admin menu; Install app is under Screen tools instead of a bar over Admin; the Printer page shows its details only
  when the printer is switched on, with a line under each switch saying what it does.

## Practice and opening

- **Practice mode** (Screen tools, or Admin → Ready to open): try out sales with your own menu in a separate
  practice till. Nothing reaches the real sales, receipt numbers, printer, drawer or books; a yellow bar and
  PRACTICE / OEFENEN on every receipt show which till you are in.
- **Ready to open** (Admin): one list that checks the server, the kitchen connection, the printer, the last backup,
  the business details, the tax rates and the example PINs before the first sale.
- **Nothing lost when the connection drops**: an order being sent or paid stays on the device; after reconnecting it
  is looked up first, so it is never sent or paid twice.

## Phones and tablets

- **`http://cactus.local/pos`**: other devices on the truck's Wi-Fi reach the till by name, also when it is
  started with `npm start`. The number address keeps working.
- **Android app** (`CactusPOS-Android-0.2.0.apk`, built with `npm run android:build`): connects to the till, stays awake, and can print a
  receipt from the phone. iPhone and iPad: add the page to the Home Screen in Safari.

## Money

- **Check and correct today's sales** before closing: Admin → Today lists every sale with search and receipt;
  **Correct** makes a linked replacement sale and keeps the original receipt as void.
- **Cash in and out of the drawer** with an amount and a reason (the morning float is a cash-in). The count at
  closing is all the cash in the drawer, float included; X and Z reports show the movements and the difference.

## Receipts

- Every part of the receipt can be switched on or off: logo, company name, address, phone, order and time,
  payment details, closing line, each message and each QR code. **Logo only** for a logo that already carries
  the name. Switched-off text and pictures stay saved. Amounts, ABB, CRIB and the receipt number always print.

## Kitchen

- **One tap per order.** Everything still to cook is on one board, oldest first, with no empty columns. Tap a ticket
  when the food is done: it goes straight to **Ready**, also on the pickup board. Ready orders shrink to their number
  in a green strip at the top; tap the number when the guest has it. **Undo** takes back either tap.
- **Three steps** (New, Preparing, Ready) is still there for whoever prefers it: Screen tools → Kitchen screen.
- **Lock this screen to the kitchen** (Screen tools → Kitchen screen): the tablet always opens on the kitchen and
  Order, Admin and the bar at the top are gone, so nobody switches away by accident. Switch it off in the same place.
- **Made for greasy fingers**: only a short tap counts, so a thumb resting on the screen or a smear does not move a
  ticket; **Wipe screen** at the top pauses touch for 20 seconds; options and notes larger and brighter.

## Admin

- Sections grouped as **Every day**, **Set up** and **Records**; on a landscape tablet they are a menu down the left.
- Every page opens with its name and what it is for; notes that only explain are calm, amber is kept for what needs
  doing. Clearer icons throughout.
- **Tax (ABB)** works for any Bonaire business: add categories from presets (6%, 8%, 7%, 0%, exempt), rename them,
  and a **Tax check** that flags a missing or unverified rate.
- **Charge ABB** switch for a business that does not charge ABB. Switching off asks for a reason; earlier sales keep
  their ABB. Whether a food truck qualifies is still a question for the accountant, so it stays on by default.
- The PyroBookKeeper link is removed. The bookkeeping CSV exports are unchanged.

## Safety

- Updated the parts the till is built on to fix known security problems. Before the setup is made, the packaged
  till runs a drill (many sales at once, restart, backup and restore) and the setup itself is installed, updated
  over a running till and removed again; the results ship beside it.

## Checked, and not checked

Checked in this build's code: 307 unit tests and 11 browser checks pass (ready orders and pickup from Order, names on
the pickup board, stock, ABB exempt on Today, new kitchen tickets, one-tap kitchen and lock, greasy-finger taps,
allergy marks and category colours, practice and readiness, receipt switches, cash drawer and settlement). The
packaged till ran its sales/restart/restore drill, and the setup was installed, updated over a running till and
removed again on the build computer.

Not yet checked: a receipt printer and cash drawer (none chosen yet); the setup of this version on another Windows
computer. The Windows firewall step of the setup needs an administrator click that the automatic checks cannot give.

This build updates the database. After it has run, or from a backup it made, an earlier version cannot open the data.
