**Release candidate.** For trying out on the truck before 0.3.0. Keep 0.2.0 at hand if something is wrong; your
sales, menu and settings stay where they are either way.

## Install or update (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

Same as 0.2.0: download **`CactusPOS-Setup-0.3.0-rc.2.exe`**, double-click, **Next**, **Next**, **Finish**. Sales,
menu, PINs and settings are kept. Windows warns about the unsigned file: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Practice and opening

- **Practice mode** (Screen tools, or Admin → Ready to open): try out sales with your own menu in a separate
  practice till. Nothing reaches the real sales, receipt numbers, printer, drawer or books; a yellow bar and
  PRACTICE / OEFENEN on every receipt show which till you are in.
- **Ready to open** (Admin): one list that checks the server, the kitchen connection, the printer, the last backup,
  the business details, the tax rates and the example PINs before the first sale.
- **Nothing lost when the connection drops**: an order being sent or paid stays on the device; after reconnecting it
  is looked up first, so it is never sent or paid twice.

## Phones and tablets

- **`http://cactus.local/pos`**: other devices on the truck's Wi-Fi reach the till by name, also when it is
  started with `npm start`. The number address keeps working.
- **Android app** (`CactusPOS-Android-0.2.0.apk`, built with `npm run android:build`): connects to the till, stays awake, and can print a
  receipt from the phone. iPhone and iPad: add the page to the Home Screen in Safari.

## Money

- **Check and correct today's sales** before closing: Admin → Today lists every sale with search and receipt;
  **Correct** makes a linked replacement sale and keeps the original receipt as void.
- **Cash in and out of the drawer** with an amount and a reason (the morning float is a cash-in). The count at
  closing is all the cash in the drawer, float included; X and Z reports show the movements and the difference.

## Receipts

- Every part of the receipt can be switched on or off: logo, company name, address, phone, order and time,
  payment details, closing line, each message and each QR code. **Logo only** for a logo that already carries
  the name. Switched-off text and pictures stay saved. Amounts, ABB, CRIB and the receipt number always print.

## Kitchen

- **One tap per order.** Everything still to cook is on one board, oldest first, with no empty columns. Tap a ticket
  when the food is done: it goes straight to **Ready**, also on the pickup board. Ready orders shrink to their number
  in a green strip at the top; tap the number when the guest has it. **Undo** takes back either tap.
- **Three steps** (New, Preparing, Ready) is still there for whoever prefers it: Screen tools → Kitchen screen.
- **Lock this screen to the kitchen** (Screen tools → Kitchen screen): the tablet always opens on the kitchen and
  Order, Admin and the bar at the top are gone, so nobody switches away by accident. Switch it off in the same place.
- **Made for greasy fingers**: only a short tap counts, so a thumb resting on the screen or a smear does not move a
  ticket; **Wipe screen** at the top pauses touch for 20 seconds; options and notes larger and brighter.

## Admin

- Sections grouped as **Every day**, **Set up** and **Records**; on a landscape tablet they are a menu down the left.
- Every page opens with its name and what it is for; notes that only explain are calm, amber is kept for what needs
  doing. Clearer icons throughout.
- **Tax (ABB)** works for any Bonaire business: add categories from presets (6%, 8%, 7%, 0%, exempt), rename them,
  and a **Tax check** that flags a missing or unverified rate. Menu prices can be typed **without ABB**; the till
  adds it and shows what the customer pays.
- **Charge ABB** switch for a business that does not charge ABB. Switching off asks for a reason; earlier sales keep
  their ABB. Whether a food truck qualifies is still a question for the accountant, so it stays on by default.
- The PyroBookKeeper link is removed. The bookkeeping CSV exports are unchanged.

## Safety

- Updated the parts the till is built on to fix known security problems. Before the setup is made, the packaged
  till runs a drill (many sales at once, restart, backup and restore) and the setup itself is installed, updated
  over a running till and removed again; the results ship beside it.

## Checked, and not checked

Checked in this build's code: 294 unit tests and 10 browser checks pass (one-tap kitchen and lock, greasy-finger
taps, practice and Sunday readiness, receipt switches, cash drawer and settlement).

Not yet checked: the one-tap kitchen and the lock on the real kitchen tablet during service; cash movements with a
real drawer; the setup of this version on Windows beyond the build computer. Some manual pictures were taken on
Linux and look slightly different in font from the others.
